## Gun Buddy Select 0.2.0

**Named gun buddies:** Normal (small double cannon), Pop-Up Gun, Floating Cannon, Quad Cannon and Random. The choices changed, so check the Gun Buddy option after updating.

**Quad Cannon fix:** it needs much more room than the others, and 0.1.1 didn't look far enough, so it came out as the normal gun buddy. It now looks further out and to the sides, and says "NO ROOM" when it gives you the normal one.

**Cycle Button** (default L): press it in game to step through the gun buddies. Each player has their own pick, and a message says which one you're on. Pick a button your controls don't use, or turn it Off.

### 0.1.1

**Fix:** a bigger gun buddy than the usual one (Type 4, and Type 4 from Random) didn't fit at the usual spot right behind your tank, so the deploy used your ammo and put nothing down. It now goes further back, and if there's still no room you get the usual gun buddy.

### 0.1.0

First version. Pick which gun buddy a normal deploy puts down (the game's own, any of the four that A+B mixes, or Random), and optionally make A+B put down your pick too.

### How to install
1. Download `btga_gunbuddy_mod.nrm` below.
2. Open the BattleTanx: Global Assault recomp and go to the Mods tab.
3. Drop the `.nrm` file in, turn the mod on and set its options.

Needs the BattleTanx: Global Assault recomp 0.1.8 or newer.
